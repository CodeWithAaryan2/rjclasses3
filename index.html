How we teach

Tutorials here are smaller and more interactive than a lecture. Students talk a topic through until it makes sense.

Doubts cleared, not skipped

Students bring what was confusing in school and work through it with the teacher.

Learning by discussion

Ideas are tried out loud, so students hear more than one way to solve a problem.

Thinking and speaking skills

Regular discussion builds thinking, listening and discussion skills.

Three boards, one centre

SSC, ICSE and CBSE students are taught to their own syllabus.

Help beyond class hours

We guide students to the right resources and stay in touch by call, chat and video.

Teachers you can call

Parents reach Dr. Rohit Jain or Smt. Neelima Jain directly, not a front desk.
